We thank the reviewers and Area Chair for identifying important ambiguities in
the optimization, masking, ablations, and real-robot protocol. We agree that
two implementation choices require correction, and that the component and
human-held evidence must be separated more carefully. Below we report the
currently auditable results and mark the missing controlled comparisons.

These isolated retrainings change one mechanism at a time. The fixed Alpha1
weight is the strongest current variant; an exploratory two-sided Fisher test
against the bounded-gate variant gives $p{=}0.0011$. We do not claim improvement
over Legacy Full until its same-protocol $N{=}20$ anchor is complete. We also
exclude the earlier $N{=}5$ hand-held pilots and unlabelled rollout files from
this table rather than pooling incompatible protocols.

\newparagraph{Q2--Q3: Loss weighting and proprioceptive masking.}
We agree that a learned scalar optimized through the same weighted denoising
objective can be driven to its lower bound. We replace it with the fixed,
bounded schedule $w(c)=\operatorname{clip}(1+c,1,2)$. Its $10/10$ preliminary
result supports retaining this correction and extending it to $N{=}20$. We
also agree that the original post-encoder whole-lowdim mask was too coarse.
The corrected routing always retains proprioception and gripper width and
masks only tactile features. Its $13/20$ result establishes usability, but not
superiority to the pending Legacy Full anchor. These are implementation
corrections rather than a defense of the original formulations.

\newparagraph{Q4: Is feature gating necessary?}
The bounded-gate variant obtains $7/20$, and qualitative traces show reluctance
to advance unless a strong visual cue is introduced. Thus the tested gate range
appears to over-suppress vision. This result rejects that parameterization, not
gating in general. We will soften the independent gating claim, select one
weaker setting using offline prediction/action traces, freeze it before robot
evaluation, and report the result even if it is negative.

\newparagraph{Q1: Is VPCE only task progress or phase?}
We implemented same-backbone Phase-DP and Progress-DP baselines, but found that
their real-time conditioning must be aligned to actual elapsed/control time;
therefore the existing pilot and unlabelled rerun files are not used as formal
evidence. We will report corrected fixed-mount $N{=}20$ comparisons. Until then,
we narrow the claim: VPCE predicts a tactile-supervised future-contact signal
online from RGB and motion, but superiority to explicit progress remains TBD.

\newparagraph{Human-held props and limitations.}
The current table is explicitly operator-held; it cannot resolve the compliance
concern. Our minimum controlled study rigidly mounts the reader and randomizes
$N{=}20$ trials each for DP-VT, Legacy Full, and the best fixed-Alpha1 variant
under identical initialization, controller, and timeout. Only one complete
roller rotation counts as success. We will also report task-specific label
calibration and target adaptation, and do not claim zero-shot transfer to unseen
tasks, objects, or viewpoints.
