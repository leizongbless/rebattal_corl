\newparagraph{Purpose} The primary purpose of the rebuttal is to correct any factual inaccuracies in the reviews and to provide clarifications or additional details specifically requested by the reviewers.

\newparagraph{Template} This year, we are providing a rebuttal template for standardization. All content (including references) must fit in one page. Please do not change the font size or spacing; rebuttals that do not adhere to the provided template will be desk rejected. 

\newparagraph{Anonymity} The rebuttal must remain anonymous. Do not include any external links (e.g., videos, code, websites).

\newparagraph{Process} You will not be able to respond to individual reviewers on OpenReview, nor can you update your paper during the rebuttal process. However, if your paper is accepted, you will have an opportunity to incorporate reviewer and Area Chair feedback into the camera-ready version.

\newparagraph{Submission} To submit your rebuttal, click the “Rebuttal” button on your paper’s OpenReview page, upload your PDF, and submit the form.
